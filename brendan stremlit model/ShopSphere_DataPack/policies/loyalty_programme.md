# ShopSphere Global: ShopSphere Rewards Loyalty Programme

## 1. Tiers
A customer's tier is based on what they spent in the last 12 months and is recalculated on the first day of each month.

| Tier | 12-month spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | under $120 | 1 point per $1 |
| Silver | $120 to $399 | 1.25 points per $1, birthday voucher |
| Gold | $400 to $899 | 1.5 points per $1, free express shipping twice a year |
| Platinum | $900 and above | 2 points per $1, free express shipping, priority support |

## 2. Points
100 points = $1 off. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

## 3. Loyalty score
`loyalty_score` (0–1) is an internal engagement index calculated by the CRM system. It is not shown to customers, and it is not the same thing as tier.
