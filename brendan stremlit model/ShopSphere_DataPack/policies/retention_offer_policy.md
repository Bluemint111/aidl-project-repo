# ShopSphere Global: Retention and Re-engagement Offer Policy

Version 3.2. Owner: CRM and Retention team. Effective 1 July 2024.

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out who may receive a retention or re-engagement offer, which offers may be used, and the limits that apply. It applies to every outbound retention contact: email, SMS, app push and WhatsApp.

## 2. Who must never be contacted
1. Customers with `do_not_contact = Y` in the customer preference centre.
2. Email offers: customers with `email_opt_in = N`. SMS offers: customers with `sms_opt_in = N`.
3. Customers with an **open High-priority support ticket** (lost parcel or payment issue). These customers must be handled by Customer Service first. A promotional message sent while their complaint is open is treated as a service failure.
4. Customers contacted with any retention offer in the last **30 days**. Offers with `counts_toward_monthly_limit = 0` in the offer catalogue are exempt.

## 3. Offer eligibility by tier
Offer codes and values are listed in `offer_catalogue.csv`.

| Tier | Offers allowed | Maximum voucher |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | OFF-NONE, OFF-FS, OFF-V10 | 10% |
| Silver | Bronze offers plus OFF-V15 | 15% |
| Gold | Silver offers plus OFF-PTS2 | 15% |
| Platinum | All offers including OFF-V20 | 20% |

OFF-SRC (service-recovery credit) may be used for any tier, but only where the customer had a confirmed service failure in the last 60 days: a late or lost delivery, or a damaged or wrong item.

## 4. Product and stock rules
1. Vouchers must not be promoted on products marked `is_promotable = No` (margin below 18%).
2. Do not feature a product in a personalised message if `stock_on_hand = 0`, or if `stock_on_hand < 50` and the next restock is more than 14 days away.

## 5. Budget
1. Each country has a weekly retention budget (`retention_budget_2024Q4.csv`). The cost of an offer is the expected redemption cost:
   - Vouchers: voucher % × customer's average order value × expected redemption rate. The redemption rate must be estimated from `retention_contact_history.csv`; if it cannot be estimated for a segment, use 10%.
   - Shipping: $6.00 × redemption rate.
   - Points: $3.00 × redemption rate.
   - Service-recovery credit: $10.00. It is always counted at full cost.
2. Unused budget does not carry over to the next week.
3. Any single offer with an expected cost above $25 needs approval from the CRM Manager.

## 6. Contact rules
1. Use the customer's `preferred_channel` and `preferred_language` where consent allows.
2. No more than 2 marketing messages of any kind per customer per week, including campaign emails.
3. Quiet period: no SMS or WhatsApp between 9 pm and 8 am, customer local time.
4. Every message must include an unsubscribe option and must not imply urgency that is not true ("last chance" only when it really is).

## 7. Approval and audit
Every retention list must be approved by a CRM Executive before it is sent. Lists generated by automated tools must record, for each customer, the reason they were selected and the offer chosen.
