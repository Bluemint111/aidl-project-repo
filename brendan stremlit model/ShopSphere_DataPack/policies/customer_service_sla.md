# ShopSphere Global: Customer Service Standards and SLA

Version 1.9. Owner: Customer Service.

## 1. Response and resolution targets
| Priority | Typical categories | First response | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Lost parcel, Payment issue | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Medium | Late delivery, Damaged or wrong item, Refund status | 12 hours | 72 hours |
| Low | Account access, Product question, Promo code, Cancel order | 24 hours | 5 days |

## 2. Escalation
1. A ticket that misses its resolution target is escalated to a Team Lead.
2. A customer with 2 or more tickets in 30 days is flagged as "at risk". The agent handling the ticket must check the customer's history before replying.
3. Platinum customers with any open High-priority ticket are escalated straight away.

## 3. Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
CSAT surveys are sent when a ticket is closed (1 = very dissatisfied, 5 = very satisfied). About half of customers respond. The team target is an average CSAT of 4.0 or above.

## 4. Coordination with Marketing
Customer Service must be able to see whether a customer received a marketing or retention offer in the last 14 days. Marketing must not send promotions to customers with an open High-priority ticket (see the Retention Offer Policy, section 2).
