# ShopSphere Global: Shipping, Delivery and Returns Policy

Version 2.4. Owner: Operations.

## 1. Shipping options
| Option | Promised delivery | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 6 business days after dispatch | Free over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 3 business days after dispatch | $9.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day after dispatch | $19.99 |

Orders are dispatched the day after payment. During the peak season (1 November to 31 December), promised dates may be extended by up to 3 days. Customers are told this at checkout.

## 2. Late deliveries
1. A delivery is **late** if it arrives after the promised date.
2. Late by 1–2 days: apologise and update tracking. No compensation is required.
3. Late by 3 or more days: refund the shipping fee on request (Express and Overnight), or offer OFF-SRC if the customer complains.
4. Two or more late deliveries in 60 days: the customer qualifies for OFF-SRC without having to complain.

## 3. Lost parcels
A parcel is treated as lost if tracking has not updated for 7 days, or if the carrier confirms loss. The customer is offered a full refund or a free replacement at their choice, and an OFF-SRC credit.

## 4. Returns and refunds
1. Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Groceries and opened beauty products cannot be returned.
2. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the return and reach the customer's account within 10 business days.
3. Damaged or wrong items: free return label, plus a replacement or refund. No questions asked within 7 days of delivery.

## 5. Carriers
SwiftPost, ParcelNow (US, UK, India), GlobalExpress (all markets), RoyalLine (UK), DeutschLog (Germany), ColisFast (France), BharatShip (India). Carrier performance is reviewed every quarter by Operations.
